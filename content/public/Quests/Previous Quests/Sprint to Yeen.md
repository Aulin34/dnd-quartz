*Far to the south, shrouded in unbroken jungle and crowned by mist, lies Yeen, known among sailors as The Island of Doom. The Crown of Albia now calls for the bravest navigators, scholars, and treasure-seekers to chart its depths, uncover its secrets, and return with discoveries worthy of royal acclaim.*

*Your endeavors may take many forms:*

-*Mapping the Unknown* – Charting rivers, cliffs, and the endless canopy.
	
-*Cataloging Flora & Fauna* – Recording plants with strange properties and beasts unseen in Albia.
    
-*Aiding Settlement Efforts* – Securing clearings, water sources, and routes for future colonists.
    
-*Recovering Rare Treasures* – Relics hidden in the jungle’s ancient ruins or buried beneath its roots.

<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Gold</span> – The rarer and more valuable the discovery, the greater the gold reward.
