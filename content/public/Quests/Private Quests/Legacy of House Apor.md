*Einar witnessed the fall of Neathshara and fled. He did not stand in defense of the World Tree, and in his absence the Empire seized Thaldorra.*

*Now, with Nereth gone, Einar moves to claim the destiny he once abandoned.*

*He seeks to ascend the throne of Neathshara, restore elven power to the realm, and fulfill Queen Zelda’s final wish: to see an Apor rule.*

*To do so, Einar calls for powerful allies to free the elves, ignite a resistance, and reclaim what was lost.*
