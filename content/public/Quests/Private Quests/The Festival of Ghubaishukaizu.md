Non-humans who have earned both respect and admiration within **[[Autumngrove]]**, has inspired countless centaurs and feyfolk across the lands. To the people of [[Dephena]], their presence is more than ceremonial; it is a symbol of hope that the old prejudices of the Empire may one day fall away.*
