> # Queen of Steelrun
> ![[steelrun_queen.png]]

*The Queen of [[Steelrun]] calls upon warriors from across the realm to seek out and eradicate the remaining dangerous beasts lurking within the Druecian continent. [[Steelrun]] has pledged to purge these threats to ensure the safety of the empire and humankind alike.*

*Adventuring groups will be tested in [[Steelrun]] to determine their strength, wit, and skills. Based on these trials, they will be assigned specific beasts to track and eliminate. These hunts are not for the faint of heart, only the most capable will be entrusted with the empire’s safety.*

---
**Difficulty**: <span style="color:rgb(0, 176, 80)">Low</span>-<span style="color:rgb(112, 48, 160)">Extreme</span>

**Rewards**: Rewards are variable based on the size/power of the beast defeated. And based on how many warriors survive the battle.

**Additional Information**: Parties typically consist of 8 to 12 members, combining strength and strategy to face the deadly challenges ahead. Prize money for each beast defeated will be divided among the surviving hunter.
