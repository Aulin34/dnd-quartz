*Beyond Albia’s southern seas lies a land of mist-wreathed shores and secrets untold. The Crown has decreed that those bold enough to brave the voyage to the Southern Continent will be richly rewarded for their discoveries. Every artifact unearthed, every rare plant cataloged, and every hidden ruin mapped will bring you closer to fame, fortune, and the King’s eternal favor.*

*Your endeavors may take many forms:*

-*Charting New Lands* – Mapping unclaimed shores, rivers, and mountain ranges
	
-*Cataloging Flora & Fauna* – - Recording species unseen in Albia, whether harmless or perilous.
    
-*Aiding Settlement Efforts* – Securing safe harbors, fertile lands, and vital resources for future colonists.
    
-*Recovering Rare Treasures* – - Relics, gems, or artifacts of remarkable value or history.

<span style="color:rgb(255, 192, 0)">Gold</span> – The rarer and more valuable the discovery, the greater the gold reward.
