	- They eavesdrop on a conversation about the Queen and the Ashen Maw, and observe various cultural artifacts.
	- They encounter a mysterious man named Jackson, who seems to be involved in some sort of scheme.
	- The group gathers information and tries to determine the intentions of the individuals they meet.

- The group meets with Sir Quinton Valerae, who offers information about [[The Ashen Maw]]'s plans and the possibility of a joint attack between [[Nyvara]] and the [[Empire of Druecia]].
	- ![[valerae_noble.png]]

	- They also discuss the possibility of a deception and the potential consequences of the diplomatic situation.

	- The group learns about the location of Meowfi's mother through [[Celine Emberfall]], Queen of Autumngrove.

	- They choose to stop at [[Grimrest]], and the cost is split among the players.

- The group flies to [[Qino]], where they hope to find answers about Meowfi's mother.
	- The group is tasked with helping the city deal with an infestation of creepy eyeball pests.
	- ![[qino_councilman.png]]
	- After Thraine was pushed into a well by a trickster, the group worked together to pull out three eyeballs from the well with a rope. 
	- They then dealt with the remaining eyeballs in the city, and the trickster revealed a massive eyeball creature attached to his back.
	- The adventurers successfully removed the creature and saved the trickster's life.

- They then left the city to explore the Cave of Love.

	- ![[leonor.png]]
	- ![[crubby.png]]
	- The group learns that the eyeball creatures are a result of a beholder's defiance.
	- They are tasked with speaking with the beholder to stop the creatures.
	- The group is warned not to make eye contact with the beholder, as this will result in being turned to stone.
	- The group accepts the quest and sets out to find the beholder.

- The group finds the beholder
	- ![[beholder.png]]

	- They try to get closer to it, but it's surrounded by an army of eyeballs. They consider using a mirror to distract it, but ultimately decide against it.
	- They discuss the beholder's appearance and personality, and consider how to deal with it.
	- They also discuss the possibility of getting married in the game. (?)

- Thraine must decide whether to use his mirror or trust the creature, whether to accept the beholder as a lover or not.
- The group must decide how to split the extra gold they've collected.
- The beholder's true nature and intentions remain uncertain. The story continues.

- The group enters the Cave of Love, where Thraine sees his father and a captured [[Roin]], who is being held by pirates.
	- ![[red_pirate.png]]
	- They exit the cave and consider visiting a place with similar trees to the ones in the cave, but ultimately decide against it.
	- Thraine and Roin part ways, and the group heads back to the ship.

- The group then heads to Qino for some shopping.
