- Thraine and Meowfi set out for [[Gorbon's Tower]].

- As they arrive, they speak to Thomas.
	- He claims that the Empire will not actually attack [[the Ashen Maw]], but instead [[The World Tree]].
	- The Empress claims that the elves are a greater threat, and stunting them of their main source of power is necessary.
	- Captain Stein has been overseeing the plans and the Empress is worried he'll spill the beans.

- The group walks up to the tower, to see a little man waiting.
- He introduces himself as [[Melvin Mibbs]], and that he has been waiting for Gorbon to answer for eight hours.

- They try to get a response from knocking at the door, to no avail. It shocks if you touch it.
- The group hoots and hollers to hear no response.
- As Thraine offers a coin, they notice a man running away from the tower.

- Thomas, Meowfi, and Melvin catch up: he claims to be "Shorbon" and is scared of the wizard.
- The group eventually gets him to admit that he is actually Gorbon.
	- ![[gorbon.png]]

- He is not actually powerful.
- He claims that the true power lies in the [[Marchwood Forest]] to the west, and that we must speak to the trees with reverence.
- Gorbon refuses to join the group so they take all of his tomatoes.

- The group heads into the forest.
- Melvin tells the rest of the group his background, of the history of the great war in [[Celestara]].
- Massive booms can be heard all around. Melvin believes these to be natural occurrences like earthquakes, but Thraine claims that the noises are caused by Ents.

- As Melvin yaps about the Ent hoax, one steps right over them. Embarrassing!
	- ![[ent.png]]
- He wants to meet with the Grandmaster Ent. He knows of the threat on the World Tree, but thinks they have a century until they can get there to defend it.

- We can speak to the Grandmaster, only under the disguise provided by the Ent. If a cure is not given in 24 hours, the disguised will become a tree forever. 
- Melvin offers himself up.
- The Ent, named [[Entberd]], drips some sap onto Melvin, who now has barklike, leafy skin.
- We all head to the Ent meeting.

- Grandmaster Tree speaks
	- ![[grandmaster_ent.png]]
	- After the death of his father, he took the "future device" and set North to defend the World Tree.
	- The future device claims that the World Tree will be attacked 100 years from now.
	- He shows the future device in his arm, and it looks like an iPad
	- It's Big Treeman, presumably the Grunk's Little Treeman from long ago.

- Grandmaster Tree spots Thraine, and grabs him out of Entberd.
- Thraine offers his mind, and Grandmaster Tree attemps to read it.

- He grows a root from his beard. Melvin takes it.
- The root allows us to communicate with the Grandmaster Tree.

- He also gives us the Fruit of the World Tree. It is extraordinarily rare and the primary ingredient of the Reincarnate spell.

- The group decides the plan to defend The World Tree.
	- Group A, consisting of Thraine and Thomas, will head north to bring [[Aetheryn]] in to help.
		- If possible, they will also head to [[the Harmonia Forest]] and [[Ashborune]] to ask them for help.
	- Group B, consisting of Meowfi and Melvin, will head south to the Orc clans to ask them to help.
		- Afterwards, they'll warn Paw'Merra, Szin-Ka Dominion, and Nuah-Ki and ask them to help.
