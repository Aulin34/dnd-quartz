- Team Empire attempts a pincer formation on the tree. They successfully obliterate the surrounding forests as well as the Paw'Merran villages.

- Team World Tree holds the line long enough for Grandmaster Tree to summon a devastating storm, which decimates one of the Empire's flanks.

- Thraine, leading hundreds of Wyverns into the skies, manages to hold his own for a short while.
	- Ultimately, he is knocked to the ground and manages to be saved by the roots of the World Tree, which takes him to its heart.

- A small Empire crew, led by Lionel, attempts to sneak past the Ents defending the Tree.
	- Scooby is killed in the process.

- It's a devastating sight for both sides.
	- While the Empire was largely successful in their offensive, they could not have anticipated the arrival of [[Suave]], who would singlehandedly obliterate thousands of airships.

- The World Tree attempts to crush Lionel's crew.
	- Before it can land the blow, Channing Tatum deploys a magic barrier which forces him to reveal his true nature in the process.
	- Channing Tatum is a powerful Tiefling magic user working for [[The Ashen Maw]].
	- The mission objective is too important to discuss this further, so they book it to the heart of the tree.

- Thraine and Lionel battle in the heart of the World Tree, Lionel loses his arm in the process.

- Suave and Channing duke it out.

- Francis Angold reveals that he's wanted to save the tree all along, and teleports Thraine away.

- Melvin tries to save Moman, while Meowfi takes Toasty.
	- Moman ultimately dies before he can be led to safety.

- Lionel and Channing fend off Suave to inject the tree with the Concord Forumla.

- Melvin arrives with enough time to teleport himself in the way of Channing.
	- While he was successful in blocking the attempt, Channing manages to drop the formula to a one-armed Lionel, who finishes the job.

- A blinding light flashes, and The World Tree falls.

- Melvin, half-conscious, is rescued by a blonde man.

- The survivors are taken to [[Blackreach Prison]].
