*Message From the Warden:*

```
For centuries, my family has overseen Blackreach. Through war, rebellion, famine, and siege, our walls have never been breached. Not once in its entire history has a single prisoner escaped our custody.

Until now.

Approximately one dozen inmates have fled my prison.

In doing so, they have shattered a legacy that stood unbroken for generations. I have made a mockery of my family’s name. I have dishonored my bloodline. That stain is mine to bear.

Effective immediately, I resign as Warden of Blackreach.

Control of the prison will be transferred to the authority of the Cinderforge. They will restore order where I have failed.

My time as Warden is finished. The purpose that defined my life is gone.

But I am not.

To the escapees who now skulk in shadows and celebrate their brief illusion of freedom, hear me clearly.

I no longer answer to a title.

I answer to vengeance.

My new purpose is singular. I will see each of you returned to Blackreach in chains. If chains cannot hold you, then graves will.

And on a personal note...

Melvin Mibbs.

If you are listening.

You humiliated me. You turned my life’s work into spectacle.

I will find you.

And I will be your end.

Enjoy whatever fleeting comfort you have stolen from this world.

It will not last.
```
