By decree of Her Majesty the Queen, we bring grave news regarding the escalating tensions on the Nyvara continent.

Reports confirm that forces of the Paw-Mera Kingdom are conducting military buildups along the colonial borders. Barricades and forward positions have been established, and the threat of further aggression grows by the hour. In response, Fort Brinsmead has mobilized its garrisons, with reinforcements already en route to strengthen our defenses.

Her Majesty issues this solemn warning: any further act of hostility directed toward the colonies will compel the Empire to answer with decisive force. The safety of our people shall not be bartered, nor will the lives of our settlers be left unguarded.

Remain vigilant, Autumngrove. Stand steadfast in unity and trust in the Crown.

For the realm. For the safety of all.
