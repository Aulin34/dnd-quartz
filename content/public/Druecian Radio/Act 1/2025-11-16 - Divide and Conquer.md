The cleanup of Honos continues steadily.

Imperial forces report strong progress, and the King of Honos has publicly expressed his gratitude for the Empire’s swift aid following the horrific terrorist attack carried out by Suave and the Cattivu's Will. His message has been repeated across the kingdom, bringing a sense of unity to a shaken people.

Across the realm, however, all conversations have turned toward the upcoming unification conference in the east. Delegates from cities and kingdoms across the region are already beginning their journeys to Faywharf, where negotiations, trade discussions, and diplomatic councils are scheduled to take place. Many eastern states have condemned the attack on Honos, yet insist that Suave was framed and that the Cattivu's Will alone is responsible. The Empire firmly rejects this claim, calling any defense of Suave a dangerous misjudgment.

This growing divide has cast uncertainty over the conference. Some hope it will strengthen cooperation among the eastern powers, while others fear it may deepen tensions with the Empire itself.

The search for Suave continues, with no verified sightings reported. Authorities urge vigilance as the investigation moves forward.

For the realm. For safety. For truth enduring.
