A Royal Herald to All Peoples of the Realm

Let it be known across the lands of Druecia and beyond that His Majesty, King Reginald Moon, in his boundless wisdom and enduring devotion to the future of Albia, now calls upon the boldest minds and bravest souls of this age!

To Scholars, Navigators, Cartographers, and Seekers of Fortune: A great and noble endeavor awaits. The southern continent, vast and veiled in ancient mystery, and the foreboding, mist-wreathed Isle of Yeen, long untouched by crown or compass, now lie within reach.

His Majesty invites all worthy and willing to join the cause of expansion, discovery, and glory. Those who dare sail beyond the known world shall be the first to plant the banner of Albia upon uncharted soil. Riches, land, and lasting renown shall be the reward of those who return triumphant.

Let none mistake this for a safe pursuit. Peril lies thick in these wild lands, but so too does greatness. Beasts of old, ancient magics, and rival banners seek the same prize. Only the resolute shall succeed.

To those whose hearts burn with ambition, whose blades are unshaken, and whose curiosity rivals their courage, Albia opens her coffers and her future.

Prove thyself in service to crown and kingdom, and your name shall echo through the halls of time.

Signed and Sealed, Reginald Moon

For the glory of Druecia and the Kingdom of Albia!
