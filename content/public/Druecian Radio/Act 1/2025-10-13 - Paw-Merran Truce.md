Attention, citizens of Autumngrove and loyal subjects of the Druecian Empire.

At last, some welcome news reaches our ears. The ceasefire between the Druecian colonies and the tribes of Paw-Mera holds strong. Trade routes that once trembled under threat now flow freely once more, and the markets of Autumngrove swell with goods once thought lost to conflict.

Reports from the Southern Continent confirm that many adventurers and settlers have returned northward, bringing exploration and expansion to a cautious standstill. Though some view this retreat as a setback, Albian officials see it for what it is, a chance to regroup, to learn, and to fortify our strength before pressing forward once more.

In brighter tidings still, an adventuring party known as Druecia’s Finest has achieved a remarkable accord with Cinderforge, a mighty kingdom within Nyvara renowned for its craftsmanship and its boundless reserves of precious metals. This new alliance promises to usher in a golden age of production and innovation, bolstering our armories and advancing the progress of the Empire itself.

Let these developments stand as proof that even in uncertain times, the will of Druecia endures. Our Queen’s vision guides every accord, every peace, and every triumph. Hold faith, citizens, the dawn yet rises over Autumngrove.

Trust in the Crown. Trust in Druecia.
