Attention, citizens of Autumngrove. This is a broadcast from the Council of Watch.


By grace and guidance of Her Majesty the Queen, we bring joyous news from the Nyvara continent. Peace has been restored! Through steadfast diplomacy and the unwavering will of the Crown, the fires of conflict have been extinguished, and the realms of Nyvara now stand united in harmony.


A new golden age of prosperity dawns for the Drueican Empire and all who dwell beneath its banner. Trade, travel, and trust may once again flow freely across the colonies, unburdened by the shadow of war.


In this moment of triumph, Her Majesty has extended her hand beyond our borders. The Queen has made an official appeal to the Kingdom of Albia, urging an end to the so-called “Crusade” upon the southern continent and of Yeen. She has spoken truly, that such ventures have brought nothing but needless loss, and the sacrifice of too many skilled adventurers.


Today, the King of Albia has answered. In a declaration of reason and restraint, he has confirmed that all expansion into Yeen and the southern continent shall cease within five days’ time. Adventurers and expeditionaries are advised to return at once for their own safety. Those who choose to remain do so of their own will, and the Crown of Albia shall bear no claim to their fate.


Let all of Autumngrove celebrate this day. A day when peace prevails, and wisdom reigns. The Queen’s vision endures, and the realm stands brighter for it.


For the realm. For the safety of all. For peace everlasting.
