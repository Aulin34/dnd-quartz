### **Overview**

Orion Starling is never one to bet against fate. He expresses this ideal strongest through his deck of Tarot Cards, which can be pulled from at any time to alter the course of the recipients life in a variety of ways.

He spent several months at [[Blackreach Prison]] with his cellmate [[Melvin Mibbs]]. There, they developed a bond which would invariably change the course of their lives. Orion joined Melvin's other friends at the prison and assisted with the group's escape.

Subsequently, he and the rest of the group were contracted by their saviors to discover the origin of the crater where Northern [[Lunaris Point]] once stood. This adventure would lead them into the depths below [[The Crying Lands]], where they discovered an elaborate bunker filled with robots.

[[The Creator]] of this bunker, fueled by his insanity, unleashed a jar of Rogue Spirits of Preservation upon Orion. This completely altered not only his physical appearance, but his inner being as well.
	Since this event, Orion seems newly motivated to spread the influence of Victor Kruslav, known today as [[The Ice King]].

After a tumultuous journey to The Ice King's domain, Orion was succinctly obliterated by him on the steps of his castle.

### **Appearance**

Orion's transformation has altered his appearance entirely. He now exhibits pale blue skin and ivory hair. He is perhaps identifiable easiest with his cloak, which is  adorned with a distinct pattern of stars.

### **Background**

Nearly nothing is known of Orion's history prior to his time at Blackreach Prison.
