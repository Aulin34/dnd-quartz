### **Overview**

Francis, after the incident in the frozen forests, found himself imprisoned within the oppressing walls of Cinderforge. For years, he was powerless to heed the guidance of The Light. His luck changed for the better after Druecia's Finest arrives in Cinderforge. Claiming world-consuming consequences if he doesn't find a mysterious dwarf, the group reluctantly allows him to tag along in exchange for his assistance in their adventures across the continents.

### **Appearance**

A pale, tired looking human. His features are sunken, eyes completely blank. His blonde hair is ragged, grown out to shoulder length. He wears simple leather clothes and straps. While not much can be said of Francis' appearance, his mysterious nature makes him compelling nonetheless. 

### **Background**

Francis was the only surviving member of the fateful expedition to the frozen forests. He remembers nothing of the incident, just that he awoke to find himself blinded and renewed with the purpose of following a mysterious force only known as "The Light".

### **Current Activity**

Joined Druecia's Finest in their adventures across the continents. The Light guides him to a particular dwarf of great intrigue.
