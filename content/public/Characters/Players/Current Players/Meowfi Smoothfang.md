### **Overview**

Joined up with Druecia's Finest, largely to help look for her mom. Found information as to the location of her mother, who is still alive but has been kidnapped by pirates.

During the chaos of the delegation of the Eastern Conclave, Druecia's Finest disbanded. Her group members [[Sylas Thorne]] and [[Dadaka]] are presumably dead.

On the run from the Empire.

### **Appearance**

A sleek, white-furred Tabaxi with piercing green eyes that gleam with sharp focus. Dressed in a dark green cloak and fitted leather armor, she carries herself with the quiet precision of a seasoned hunter.

Her slender build, combined with the elegant sweep of her tail, gives them an agile and graceful presence, embodying both the lethality and elegance of a ranger.

### **Background**

Fled from the burning of her village at a young age.
