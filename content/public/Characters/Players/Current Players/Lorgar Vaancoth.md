### **Overview**

Lorgar is a strange dragonborne man. He joined Druecia's Finest after stalking them throughout the jungles of [[Yeen]]. His motives, if he has any, are unknown to the group.

Lorgar came to Yeen by a small dinghy. He tried to ferry Druecia's Finest north, but was unsuccessful. He spent the following night murdering and eating [[King Monkey]].
