### **Overview**

On his way to [[Dupperville]], Willis found himself kidnapped by a band of mercenaries from [[Honos]]. His knowledge of the last known location of [[Suave]] quickly turned him into a bargaining chip between the group from Honos and Druecia's Finest. After [[Lionel Caldwell]] negotiated his release, Willis spent the better part of a day asleep on [[Rusty Ruddleson]]'s ship.

During the lava worm attack in Honos, Willis awoke, utterly confounded with everything going on. Yet, he sprung into action, helping Rusty and the group to set sail and fight off a horde of Imps.

His fighting prowess made him valuable to the group, and they agreed to take him on if he could continue to prove his worth.

### **Appearance**

Willis is a Harengon featuring dark fur. He has a lean build, and wears a loose purple Gi.
