### **Overview**

In a last-ditch effort to save Celestara from ruin, Melvin set out to [[Gorbon's Tower]] hoping to find the powerful wizard. Instead, he found that any notion of such a powerful wizard was a complete farce by [[Gorbon]] himself.

He now finds himself traveling with the remaining members of the now fugitive group Druecia's Finest. To save the world, it's going to take all they've got.

### **Appearance**

A squat little halfling man with a pleasant expression. Dons an elegant greyed moustache.

His armor is seemingly of an unknown land, with silver stars firmly arranged over the dark blue trim.  A matching silver star can be seen on a necklace he never takes off.

### **Background**

Melvin, born a farmer in the outskirts of Valconia, lived a simple life with his father. He worked the land as hard as he could, spending all his free time plucking away at the mandolin his father gave him. Music was his solace.

Farm life in Southern Valconia differed from the relative comforts of places like [[Cornhall]]; here, the threat of [[The Burning Expanse]] loomed over everything. It wasn't enough to grow crops. His father had to teach him how to defend himself as well. Occasionally, able hands were called to defend the Wall of [[Celestara]] from demonic forces. It was there, amidst the chaos, that Melvin learned to weave magic into his defense. It wasn't easy, but they did what they could to get by.

Now an adult, Melvin has become a cornerstone of the Celestaran community. He runs a tavern named after his late father. 'Mibbs', situated near the wall, provides much-needed respite for those who risk their lives defending the city. He doesn't run the show alone, however; [[Bentley]] tends the bar when Melvin needs to step away to entertain the crowd or handle business.

Yet, the unrelenting fight against the demonic forces is always on his doorstep. With rumors spreading of the Druecian Empire’s growth and their plot to destroy [[The World Tree]], Melvin has decided to take it upon himself to set things right. Godspeed, little man.
