### **Overview**

Francis, after the incident in the [[Glacier Hollow]], found himself imprisoned within the oppressing walls of Cinderforge. For years, he was powerless to heed the guidance of The Light. His luck changed for the better after Druecia's Finest arrives in Cinderforge.

Claiming world-consuming consequences if he doesn't find a mysterious dwarf, the group reluctantly allows him to tag along in exchange for his assistance in their adventures across the continents.

Pretended to aid in the destruction of the World Tree, but was ultimately focused on finding and rescuing the special dwarf.
	Found his special dwarf in the heart of the World Tree, right before its destruction.
	Took him to a shadowy realm with a horrifying creature, and then left.

Saved by the special dwarf after the tree fell.

Spent the following months in [[Blackreach Prison]] with his cellmate [[Rango]].

Escaped Blackreach Prison, where he has seemingly fallen into a perpetual slumber aboard Rusty's ship.

### **Appearance**

A pale, tired looking human. His features are sunken, eyes completely blank. His blonde hair is ragged, grown out to shoulder length. He wears simple leather clothes and straps.

Francis' left side is completely marred in shadow, while the other side glistens with a radiant glow.

### **Background**

Francis was the only surviving member of the fateful expedition to Glacier Hollow.

He remembers nothing of the incident, just that he awoke to find himself blinded and renewed with the purpose of following a mysterious force only known as "The Light".
