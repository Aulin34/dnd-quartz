### **Appearance**

Stern-looking orc with green skin, sharp features, and a thick, curled black mustache that gives him a distinguished air. He wears a brown leather aviator cap with large goggles perched on top, suggesting a rugged, adventurous lifestyle. His attire consists of a brown leather coat layered over a collared shirt and vest, secured with buckles and straps.

### **Background**

Born from what he calls “the Pit,” a sacred chasm he believes gives life to all orcs, he’s become obsessed with its mysteries. As of late, it has stopped providing, leaving his people starving. Though simple-minded, Dadaka’s loyalty and kindness toward his friends shine through his otherwise brutish nature. When the clan decided someone had to leave and seek answers, they dressed him in eccentric clothes to help him blend in with outsiders. Now, with his leather coat and pilot’s goggles, Dadaka sails across the sea, clumsily navigating foreign lands in search of a way to restore the Pit and save his people.

### **Current Activity**

Met [[Rusty Ruddleson]], a pilot in [[Albia]]. He's found himself traveling with the people he's met on Rusty's ship in search of fortune for his clan.
