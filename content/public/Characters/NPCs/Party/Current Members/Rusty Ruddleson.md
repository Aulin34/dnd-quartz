### **Overview**

- Rusty Ruddleson is a cheeky, charismatic pilot and one of Dreucia’s most daring skyfarers.

- He’s flown the crew across the realm, chasing adventure, excitement, and the occasional romantic escapade with beings of every shape and story.

- Beneath his easy grin, Rusty hides a tangle of vices and vulnerabilities, a self-professed sex addict with lingering daddy issues and a tendency to lean on hard substances when life hits turbulence.

- Despite his flaws, he’s fiercely loyal to the crew and never hesitates to stand by them when trouble comes calling.

- Rusty has a complicated relationship with his father, [[Rudolph Ruddleson]].

- Assisted the party during the battle of The World Tree.
	- Took several airships with his father, allowed the Orcs to make it in time for the battle.

- Crashed his ship into Tucker, Thomas, Lionel, and Channing.
	- He was presumed dead for months.

- Four months after the battle, Rusty made his grand re-appearance with [[Glimby]], who crashed into [[Blackreach Prison]] to help the group escape.

- Currently working with Druecia's Finest out of the [[Resistance Base]].
