### **Overview**

Roin began his journey as little more than a stowaway aboard Rusty’s ship, initially treated as a sort of adorable mascot rather than a true member of the crew. But over time, it’s become clear that there’s far more to this curious goblin than meets the eye.

His past remains shrouded in mystery, yet Roin possesses the uncanny ability to perceive the strange curse that binds many of the crew together.

Even more startling, he’s revealed flashes of potent magic, stepping forward to protect his companions through the Amethyst Vale and of Cinderforge.

Despite the secrets that surround him, Roin’s heart is unmistakably kind, and his affection for Meowfi and Thraine runs deep.
