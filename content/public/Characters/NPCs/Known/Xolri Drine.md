### **Overview**

Xolri often acts as an antagonistic force to [[Sylas Thorne]], fueled by their long and often wrought history. While this history is largely left unspoken, his current activities are difficult to ignore. He monitors and often impedes Sylas' journeys throughout the continent on behalf of Cattivu's Will.

### **Notes**

- Member of Cattivus Will.

- Hates Sylas and tried to kill him when they were younger.

- Casted the spell that killed [[Odell Aerisveil]].

- Accelerated Sylas’ “Process” in [[Grimrest]].
