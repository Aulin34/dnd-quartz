### **Overview**

Orin Aerisveil was born into the esteemed Aerisveil family, a proud line of Air Genasi sworn to protect the city of [[Aetheryn]].

Unlike his gifted siblings, Orin came into the world without the family’s innate magical power, earning him only pity and scorn from his peers.

Determined to prove his worth, he dedicated himself to the sword, teaching himself the art of combat through relentless practice and sheer will. Though his heart is noble and his intentions pure, Orin’s efforts to help often land him in more trouble than triumph.

After the brutal assassination of his sister Odell at the hands of Cattivu's Will, Orin’s once-gentle resolve hardened into a vow of vengeance - a promise he intends to keep, no matter the cost.
