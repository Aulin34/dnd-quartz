- Captain of the ship that allowed the group to escape from [[Blackreach Prison]].

- Deploys a bubble around his ship, providing a sturdy enough air pocket for the ship to sail underwater.
	- Can pop the bubble to shoot objects off it at incredibly fast speeds.

- Coordinated the prison escape with [[Rusty Ruddleson]] in exchange for the group's assistance in helping them discover the origin of The Crying Lands, where Northern[[Lunaris Point]] once stood.
