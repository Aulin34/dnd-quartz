### **Overview**

Kakada is the bright-skinned, bright-spirited son of Dadaka, born under the strangest of circumstances.

After Dadaka consumed a mysterious orange in the town of Dupperville, his skin turned the same vivid hue and soon after, he somehow gave birth to his half-orc, half-orange child.

From the moment he was born Kakada has idolized his father, seeing him as the greatest hero who ever lived.

Fiercely loyal and endlessly curious, he strives to follow in Dadaka’s footsteps in every way he can. Yet there’s something unusual about Kakada beyond his color he seems to grow and mature faster than most, a trait perhaps tied to his peculiar orange lineage.

What kind of man he’ll become remains a mystery.
