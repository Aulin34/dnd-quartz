### **Overview**

Thomas Glindley is the quieter and far more reserved half of the renowned Glindley Twins. In stark contrast to his flamboyant brother, Thomas is a man of few words and deadly focus.

His name carries both fame and infamy, forever shadowed by the whispered tale of his younger brother’s murder, a crime that made him as feared as he is respected.

Where Tucker wields his sword with flair, Thomas prefers the precision of the gun, and few alive can match his aim.

Efficient, unflinching, and ruthlessly decisive, Thomas rarely wastes a shot or a word. When danger strikes, he’s often the first to act, ending fights before they even begin.

After learning of the attempt on his brother's life, he swore to take revenge. Furious at Sylas and Dadaka.

Knew of plans to apprehend the group, including Thraine, who he greatly respected.

Tried to warn Thraine of the dangers and to lay low, but he did not listen.

As chaos erupted durng the Eastern Conclave, Thomas narrowly managed to escape with his life and is now a fugitive to the Empire.

On the run with the remaining members of Drueicia's Finest.
