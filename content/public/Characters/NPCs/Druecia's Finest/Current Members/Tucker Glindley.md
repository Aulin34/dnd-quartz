### **Overview**

Tucker Glindley is one half of the famed Glindley Twins, though by all accounts he’s the more popular and charismatic of the duo.

Strikingly handsome and effortlessly eccentric, Tucker lives for the spotlight and carries himself with an air of glamour wherever he goes.

A showman even in battle, he shuns blackpowder weapons as crude and inelegant, preferring the artistry of the sword and few can match his skill with one.

Beneath his cocky grin lies a true warrior’s heart; when the fight turns dire, there’s no ally more reliable or deadly to have at your side than Tucker Glindley.
