### **Overview**

Kakada was the bright-skinned, bright-spirited son of [[Dadaka]], born under the strangest of circumstances.

After Dadaka consumed a mysterious orange in the town of [[Dupperville]], his skin turned the same vivid hue and soon after, he somehow gave birth to his half-orc, half-orange child.

From the moment he was born in the caves of the [[Frozen Forests]], Kakada has idolized his father, seeing him as the greatest hero who ever lived.

Fiercely loyal and endlessly curious, he strove to follow in Dadaka’s footsteps in every way he can. Yet there was something unusual about Kakada beyond his color. He seemed to grow and mature faster than most, a trait perhaps tied to his peculiar orange lineage.

In a rather predictable turn of fate, his rapid aging would become his demise. After spending too much time in an ancient pyramid's time acceleration chamber, the once-shimmering light of life within Kakada would quickly dim, leaving a legacy to be remembered for centuries to come.
