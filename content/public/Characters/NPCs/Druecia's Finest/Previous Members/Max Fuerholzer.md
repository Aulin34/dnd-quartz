### **Overview**

Max Fuerholzer is a humble mouseman who first crossed paths with Dadaka in the city of [[Suncrest]].

Driven by a desire to support his family back in [[Albia]], Max set out to explore the southern continent, seeking both adventure and opportunity. Those who traveled with him knew him as a kind-hearted soul, brave, selfless, and always ready to lend a helping hand.

But after the crew’s visit to the elven city of [[Moonspire]], Max mysteriously vanished, leaving his companions to wonder what fate befell their steadfast friend.
