### **Overview**

Mike Ro Fiber was a streetwise hustler from [[Albia]] who first crossed paths with Sylas while trying to sell him a handful of so-called supergrass.

For reasons known only to Sylas, Mike was invited aboard Rusty’s ship, where he quickly became both a nuisance and a source of questionable entertainment. Found getting high with Rusty and stirring up trouble, Mike’s easygoing nature masked a darker truth.

He was secretly a spy for Cattivu's Will, tasked with watching Sylas’s every move.

His double life eventually caught up to him, ending in a fatal encounter with Dadaka and a Sun Elf.
