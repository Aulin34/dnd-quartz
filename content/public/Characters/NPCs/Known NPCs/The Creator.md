### **Notes**

- Once known as Linden Kruslav, The Creator's descent into madness began after his father's transformation into [[The Ice King]].

- Invented blackpowder weaponry, which invariably changed the realm forever.
	- Assassinated Tybus Fran, then ruler of the realm out of [[Autumngrove]]
	- The invention served to counter the rule of elven magic in across the realm, carving the way for the Emberfall dynasty.

- Discovered the true nature of [[Celine Emberfall]], and was subsequently banished to spend the rest of his days in a bunker of his own creation underneath [[Lunaris Point]].
	- Named "The Creator's World"

- In an effort to revive his father, The Creator caused a massive explosion which decimated the northern half of Lunaris Point, and the region is known today as [[The Crying Lands]].

- After transferring Rogue Spirits of Preservation into [[Orion Starling]], The Creator triggered a self destruct on his bunker, killing himself in the process.
