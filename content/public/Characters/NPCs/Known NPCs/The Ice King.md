### **Notes**

- Once known as Victor Kruslav

- At the command of Tybus Fran, a rising elven power broker, Victor was drafted to sail with a crew of twelve into the uncharted Northern Arctic Shelf.
	- He would be gone for three years, leaving the thirteen-year-old Linden to run the forge and provide for his family and neighbors.
	- During the voyage, a violent feud broke out between two crewmen.
	- Victor, attempting to intervene, was thrown overboard into the freezing waters.
	- By the time the crew dragged him back aboard, his skin had turned an unnatural pale blue, and jagged shards of glowing ice had embedded themselves in his flesh.
	- They diverted to a nearby island in a desperate bid to save him, but Vander never awoke.
	- His heart had stopped. Believing him dead, they prepared to bury him the next morning.
	- That night, something unnatural occurred. Vander’s hair turned stark white, his eyes glowed with an eerie, frosted light.
	- He rose without a word and moved among the sleeping crew, his frostbitten hands freezing each man solid, their bodies turned to lifeless ice as if their very souls had been ripped away.
	- Those who woke to stop him were no match for his newfound power. Commanding ice with terrifying strength, Vander declared himself the Ice King, sparing a handful of survivors and allowing them to flee as a great citadel of frost rose behind him.

- Killed [[Orion Starling]].
