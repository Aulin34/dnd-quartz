### **Notes**

- Raised by Ministers in the Temple of Suave. They believe that Suave is the first coming of a new set of Gods to the Realm.

- Said to be born from a Dragons Corpse after the battle of Proteus.

- Popular in Eastern Druecia. A Popular hero who has aided the people there.

- Ventured to [[Honos]] to Help cure the disease. Offered himself on prisoner when he learned the people's distrust of him and magic.

- Fixed Lionel's back.

- Saved the Kings Life after he was stabbed by [[Patient Zero]].

- Saved [[Thraine Axegrinder]] and [[Tucker Glindley]] from [[The Scourge]].

- Left Honos to allow Druecia's Finest to take credit.

- Went into hiding after hearing about his bounty.
