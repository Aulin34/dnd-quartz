### **Notes**

- Skips is a strong, loyal member of the Northern Monkey Tribe of Yeen, ruled by [[King Monkey]].

- Skips joined the group headed north on [[Lorgar Vaancoth]]'s dinghy.
	- After running into trouble on the way out, [[Paul]] and Skips had to dive into the water to try and save the group. They never resurfaced.

- Their true fate is uncertain, though Paul and Skips are likely dead.
