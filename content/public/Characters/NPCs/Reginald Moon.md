### **Notes**

King Reginald “Reggie” Moon is desperately attempting to restore Albia’s former glory.

He believes that the kingdom’s revival depends on expanding into new, prosperous territories, hoping that fresh conquests and colonies will bring increased wealth and influence.

To this end, Reggie has generously funded explorers, adventurers, and settlers to seek out and claim new lands, particularly focusing on establishing colonies in the recently discovered southern continent and on Yeen.

His strategy seemed promising when explorers founded Suncrest on this new continent. However, Reggie’s efforts are complicated by competing settlements from powerful Kingdoms such as [[Autumngrove]] and [[Grimrest]], who have also begun colonizing the region.

Determined not to lose this critical opportunity, Reggie continues to invest heavily, offering substantial rewards to adventurers who can quickly claim and settle new territories.
