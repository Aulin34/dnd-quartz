### **Notes**

- A bounty from [[Steelrun]] was put on his head after he traveled to Druecia.
	- Was just trying to escape the froghemoth chasing him. Very sad.

- Fought with Druecia's Finest and the Steelrun mercenary group to kill the froghemoth, and was ultimately successful.

- Posesses tears with healing properties.
	- [[Sheen]], remembering this fact, used the tears to revive [[Sylas Thorne]] (for better or for worse)
	- Tears were also used to revive Tucker Glindley after his assassination.
	- Dadaka poked him in the eye to get some tears, causing him to attack.

- After trying to defend himself, he was cannoned to death by Dadaka, [[Rusty Ruddleson]], and [[Bombshell]].
