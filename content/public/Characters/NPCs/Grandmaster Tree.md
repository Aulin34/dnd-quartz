### **Notes**

- Leader of the group of Ents that make up the [[Wimblegrove Forest]], an ever-moving forest headed to [[The World Tree]].

- Hundreds of years old, originated as a magical sapling belonging to Grunk Mageman.

- Embedded with an iPad in his arm, which told him of the impending attack on the World Tree (although the date was wrong).

- Grows a rare blue flower out of his butt which can prevent the Ent disguise from turning you into a tree.
