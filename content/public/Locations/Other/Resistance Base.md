Nearly nothing is known of the Resistance Base.

It is situated on an island between [[Port Wayne]] and [[The Burning Expanse]].

The Resistance within is led by Albia's [[Lawrence Shmud]].

It's an incredibly diverse population, largely formed after the destruction of [[The World Tree]] as a response to the Empire's increasingly violent grip on the realm.
