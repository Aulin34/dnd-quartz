The island of Yeen, though massive, is almost entirely uninhabitable. Aside from the [[Chiktalka Tribe]] who reside at the island’s southern tip, no other known group has established a settlement. The discovery of Yeen is extremely recent—explorers only stumbled upon it within the last few years. The first maps used by Druecia’s expedition teams were provided by the Chiktalka themselves.

To explorers and scholars alike, Yeen is a dream come true. The island is overflowing with magical energy and rich, untapped resources. The Kingdoms of Druecia have invested heavily in its exploration, eager to harvest its bounty. They believe that if they can harness Yeen’s power, it could give them unrivaled dominance over the rest of the realm.

But there is one major problem: Yeen is deadly.

The island is home to man-eating flora, venomous animals, treacherous storms, and nearly impassable terrain. The Chiktalka speak of terrifying legends tied to specific parts of the island—stories of ancient creatures, wild magic, and forces beyond comprehension. So far, Druecian explorers have only managed to map and investigate the northern coast of Yeen. Any attempt to push farther inland has ended in catastrophe.

One exploration team unknowingly landed in a nest of venomous spiders. The creatures encased the crew in acidic webs that melted flesh away, then used the bones to lay their eggs. Another group ventured near the island’s mountains but was driven off by enormous predatory birds. Even expeditions guided by the Chiktalka were swallowed by an impenetrable fog—no one who entered it ever returned.

Still, the danger hasn’t deterred the ambitious. Despite the mounting horror stories, explorers continue to brave the jungle. Many now believe that the island itself is alive—interconnected, intelligent, and fiercely protective of something hidden deep within. The farther one travels into its heart, the more perilous the journey becomes.

Mysteriously, Yeen has rapidly begun to expand, sprawling and consuming its surrounding oceans. It is only a matter of time before this expanse reaches the mainland. The catalyst of Yeen's growth is entirely unknown, though it is suspected that the destruction of The World Tree played some part.
