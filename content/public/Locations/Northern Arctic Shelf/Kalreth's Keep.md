The Ice Dragon Keldreth dwells in the frozen north, deep within a massive mountain range of jagged ice and stone—formed by her own magic. Unlike her brother Pyraxius, Keldreth harbors no trust for humans or the people of Druecia. She refuses to forgive those responsible for the near-extinction of her kind.

Cautious and reclusive, Keldreth rarely leaves her domain, relying instead on her loyal underlings to gather information about the outside world. Hidden within the mountains she created lies a deadly dungeon, filled with brutal traps and ancient monsters. At its end rests a powerful artifact—one she believes only a worthy warrior can claim.

Keldreth waits patiently, believing that when the right champion finally survives the dungeon and takes the artifact, they will stand beside her in a icy campaign of vengeance against Druecia—for her fallen brothers and sisters.

While most fear her wrath, the keep closest to her domain maintains a fragile trade alliance with Faywharf, a port city more tolerant of magic users and dragons alike.
