> # Estate of House Valerae
> ![[housevalerae_icon.jpg]]

House Valerae is the wealthiest and most influential noble house in Almera. Led by Escanor Valerae, their estate is the largest mansion in the known world, a testament to their immense wealth. This fortune was built upon the resource-rich lands surrounding their home.

**REDACTED** 

In recent years, the council has stripped House Valerae of the privilege to exploit the resources of the Harmonia Forest, further diminishing their power. 

Publicly, the Valeraes present themselves as staunch defenders of democracy and the common people, but in truth, they are quietly scheming to restore their former dominance and reclaim the power they once held over the realm.
