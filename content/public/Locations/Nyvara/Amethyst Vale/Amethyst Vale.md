The Amethyst Vale is a mystical land shaped by the ancient Sky Gods. Most of the Vale remains uninhabited, aside from small, scattered clans of Harengons and gnomes. Its dense forests and jagged mountains make travel treacherous, and the waters that flow through the Vale are said to possess strange magical properties.

The Vale is home to countless mysterious creatures, many of which originate from the Feywild. Travelers from Druecia have attempted to navigate the river that cuts through the Vale, but few have ever succeeded. Unseen forces seem to guard the region, turning back intruders or drawing them into peril.

One notorious case involved a pirate crew that attempted to cross the river. Their ship struck the jagged rocks beneath the surface and quickly sank. Witnesses claim to have seen strange, shadowy figures rise from the depths where the ship went down, though no one has dared to investigate further.

Legends speak of a hidden magical dungeon deep within the Vale’s mountains, rumored to be filled with untold riches, powerful artifacts, and ancient secrets. Though many adventurers have sought it, none have returned with proof of its existence.

At the heart of the Vale lies the city of Aetheryn, whose people are fiercely protective of their land. Despite their strength, the people of Aetheryn are known for being peaceful and reasonable — few would dare provoke them without first attempting to negotiate terms.
