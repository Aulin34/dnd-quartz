Gull’s Landing has grown rapidly in recent years, becoming the largest new colony on Nyvara established by the Kingdoms of Druecia. It thrives as a bustling hub of markets and piracy, serving as the primary port for Druecia’s expansion deeper into the continent. While officially aligned with Druecian interests, the city is effectively controlled by the pirates who have made it their home.

The Druecian rulers granted the pirates control over Gull’s Landing, funding their operations in exchange for raids along Nyvara’s coastline. This arrangement has made the city a center of trade and lawlessness. While Druecian laws technically apply, the pirate leaders often overlook infractions if they serve their interests.

Gull’s Landing is remarkably diverse, populated mostly by humans but also home to species from across the realm. Its markets are famous for offering exotic goods, rare trinkets, and unique foods from all corners of the world. Despite the underlying lawlessness, the city’s thriving trade and vibrant culture make it a magnet for explorers, merchants, and fortune-seekers alike.
