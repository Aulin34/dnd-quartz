Ashborune is a large village nestled in the heart of the Ember Woods, home to a strong community of Red Dragonborn who live under the direct rule of Pyraxius, the great red dragon. The Dragonborn in Ashborune are renowned for their warmth and hospitality, welcoming visitors from near and far. While the village is largely isolated from the larger political conflicts in the world, the people are deeply committed to preserving the Ember Woods and supporting their dragon ruler.

REDACTED

Pyraxius believes that peaceful coexistence with humanity is still possible. However, most of the remaining dragons disagree with him, preferring isolation to avoid further conflict.

Ashborune is especially famous for its extraordinary cuisine, with chefs renowned across the realm for their ability to prepare rich, juicy meats found only in the Ember Woods. The town’s economy thrives on trade, especially with explorers heading to the Western Ocean. Despite being ruled by a fierce, fire-breathing dragon, Ashborune is known as one of the safest and most welcoming places in Nyvara.
