The Ember Woods are deeply tied to the ancient Draconic gods. The land is ruled and fiercely protected by Pyraxius, the great red dragon of flames — one of the last remaining dragons in the realm. The trees are a deep crimson red, and the fauna within the woods are entirely fireproof, adapted to the intense heat that radiates from the land. The Ember Woods are home to many mythical creatures, including griffins and firebirds. Many explorers and anthropologists have met with the Fire Dragon clan of Dragonborns who inhabit these lands. Despite the presence of powerful creatures, the Ember Woods remain mostly peaceful and calm. These explorers have become strong advocates for protecting the woods and preserving their balance. Fortunately, the Ember Woods are naturally shielded by a mountain range along the eastern side, making it difficult for colonies from Druecia to reach the region.
