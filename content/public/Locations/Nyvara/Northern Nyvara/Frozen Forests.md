The Frozen Forests are infamous for their brutal, unforgiving climate. It is said that many from the Kingdom of Almera have been banished to these icy woods, never to be seen or heard from again. The forests are nearly impossible to reach, as they are surrounded by miles of frozen ocean, leaving them cut off from the rest of the world.

Few creatures can survive the harsh conditions, making food scarce and life nearly unsustainable. No known clans or villages have ever been established in the region, leaving it cold and desolate.

Curious explorers have occasionally ventured into the Frozen Forests, but none have stayed for long. Those who return speak of an unsettling stillness — an unnatural silence broken only by the feeling of a presence that does not welcome them. Though rough maps have been drawn of the region, much about the Frozen Forests remains shrouded in mystery and fear.
