The land surrounding Lake Drakmyr is home to roaming clans of Githyanki, who revere the lake and the great sea serpent that dwells within its depths. Every full moon, the Githyanki gather along the shores to offer food and gifts to the serpent, believing it to be a sacred protector and a bringer of fortune.

The lake is encircled by steep, jagged mountains that are treacherous to climb. Few forms of life can survive the harsh conditions at such high altitudes, and little is known about what might dwell beyond the rocky peaks.

Scattered among the Githyanki are small groups of water genasi who seem to have an unusual connection to the lake. On certain nights, they have been seen diving into the lake’s dark waters, often vanishing for days at a time before reappearing unharmed. Their motives remain a mystery, though some believe they communicate directly with the serpent.

Over the years, many explorers have attempted to uncover the secrets of Lake Drakmyr. However, none have succeeded — the serpent has swiftly and mercilessly destroyed every expedition force that dared to enter its territory.
