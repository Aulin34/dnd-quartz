> # Blackreach Prison
> ![[blackreachprison_icon.jpg]]

Blackreach is the most notorious and unforgiving prison in the realm. No one escapes Blackreach — ever. It is where the most dangerous and vile criminals, along with monstrous creatures, are sent to be forgotten. Many of the world’s kingdoms even pay Cinderforge to house their most dangerous prisoners there, ensuring they will never see the light of day again.

The prison is overseen by the Warden, a massive Goliath towering over ten feet tall. He wields Dragon's Bane, a legendary battleaxe with untold magical properties. The Warden takes immense pride in his perfect record — not a single prisoner has ever escaped under his watch. Whether this claim is true or simply believed out of fear, no one has dared to challenge it.

The island itself is as dangerous as the prison. Powerful beasts, specially trained to track and capture escapees, roam the rocky terrain. Even if a prisoner manages to slip past the guards, the island's creatures ensure that freedom remains nothing more than a fleeting dream. Blackreach is not just a prison — it is a death sentence.
