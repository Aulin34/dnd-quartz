Despite the unknown or otherwise secretive nature that defines much of the southern continent, Suncrest stands out as a bustling, lively exception. Beings of all walks of life can be found in the sprawling streets and market centers of Suncrest. Airships dominate its skies, carrying tourists from the furthest corners of Druecia and Nyvara.

Among them, [[Rusty Ruddleson]] and the group who would soon become known as Druecia's Finest would find themselves here, hoping to learn as much as they could of the city as well as the rest of the continent.

Suncrest is one of three main cities of the southern continent. To the east, the secretive city of [[Moonspire]], home to the Moon Elves, can be found. To the west, past a cursed valley nestled within a steep mountain range, is the city of [[Celestara]]. 

A friendly Suncrest man named Tyrese would tell the group more information of the surrounding area, particularly:

- South of Suncrest is mostly forest.
- Southeast contains largely jungle, with plains past it.

Notably, he mentions that no one has come back from exploring region to the southwest.

With the race to provide the [[King Reginald Moon]] with more information on the southern continent well underway, nothing is known of the current fate of Suncrest or its neighboring cities.
