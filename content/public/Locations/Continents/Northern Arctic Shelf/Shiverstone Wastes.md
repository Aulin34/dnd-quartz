It is believed that these lands were once vibrant and full of life—lush forests, thriving cities, and powerful civilizations. But all of that changed when the dragon Keldreth made the region her home. Since her arrival, the land has become a desolate, frozen wasteland. Snow and ice now cover everything, and all that remains of the once-great civilization are shattered ruins and scattered bones buried beneath the frost.

Explorers who have dared to venture into the icy wastes often report disturbing experiences. Many suffer from vivid hallucinations, plagued by dark visions of their deepest fears. Others feel an unnatural pull, an overwhelming urge to travel farther north—toward the ancient forests and the towering, frozen mountains where Keldreth is said to dwell.

Though the region is believed to be lifeless, one expedition claimed to have seen massive packs of strange creatures roaming the distant coastline. But when they tried to investigate, there was nothing to be found—no tracks, no signs, as if the creatures had vanished into thin air.
