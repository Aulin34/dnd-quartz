> # Glacier Hollow
> ![[glacierhollow_icon.jpg]]

In the far north lies the Glacier Hollow. Entry is strictly forbidden—no explorers are allowed near it. In the past, a team from Druecia attempted to uncover its secrets. Only one man returned alive: Francis Angold, a photographer who joined the expedition.

Francis brought back photographs depicting horrors beyond comprehension—his crew torn apart, their bodies mutilated beyond recognition. He returned blind and mute. Accused of murdering his team, Francis was imprisoned, though no evidence ever proved he was responsible. Strangely, every official who investigated the case died soon after from an unexplained illness.

After these events, Druecian leadership declared Glacier Hollow off-limits indefinitely. They deemed it too dangerous to explore further without a better understanding of the forces within.

Francis remains under constant surveillance in prison. He sits silently for weeks at a time—except during full moons. On those nights, he suffers violent episodes, sobbing uncontrollably and slamming his head against the stone walls. Guards have since been ordered to restrain him during the full moon to prevent injury.

Glacier Hollow remains an unsolved and terrifying mystery. No one dares venture near it now. Not even the bravest.
