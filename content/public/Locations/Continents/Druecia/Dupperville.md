If you're looking for a place to let loose, Dupperville is the destination. Known for its nonstop revelry, this infamous town boasts more taverns than homes, earning its reputation as the ultimate party capital of the realm.

At the heart of the city stands the legendary Gribble’s Bar—a must-visit landmark famed for its extremely potent liquors, beers, and exotic rums. Leaders, adventurers, and travelers from all walks of life come to Dupperville to escape their burdens, unwind, and indulge in the city's pleasures.

Laws here are few and loosely enforced, and Dupperville thrives on its hedonistic culture of alcohol, vice, and freedom. But with such freedom comes risk.

Visitors are strongly advised to take precautions during any intimate encounters, as Dupperville has gained a notorious reputation for its strange and persistent sexually transmitted diseases—some of which are rumored to be magical or even parasitic in nature. Local humor calls for travelers to "bring your piggut protection," a crude but common saying referring to makeshift safety gear sold by street vendors.

Despite the dangers, Dupperville remains one of the most colorful and uninhibited cities in the realm—a place where anything can happen and often does.
