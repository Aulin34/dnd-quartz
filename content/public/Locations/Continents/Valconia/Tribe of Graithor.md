Neighboring [[Celestara]], the Tribe of Graithor is a small colony with a great purpose.

One of the few remaining dragons in the realm rests, crested deep within the mountains to the north.

It's purified heart is suggested to bear great power.

Recent reports suggest [[Suave]] has been seen in the area defending the dragon from unknown threats.
