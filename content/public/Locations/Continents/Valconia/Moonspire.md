Druecia's Finest encountered the hidden city of Moonspire early in their travels. Almost instantly upon entering the city, the group was apprehended by the [[Moon Knight]], who would not disclose any information about himself or the city.

The Moon Knight appeared to possess magical abilities, transporting the entire group to the throne room of Moonspire.

In the throne room, the [[Queen of Moonspire]] told the group of their ancient conflict with the neighboring city of [[Suncrest]].

Additionally, she divulged the importance of [[Celestara]], the last of the three main cities on the southern continent. She claims Celestara is all that stands in the way between the southern continent and the rest of the world. 

While nothing of its existence nor history had been known prior to the group's arrival, word of the once well-kept secret of Moonspire soon spread to Druecia and Nyvara. Interested parties would soon race to stake their claim on Moonspire, along with the rest of the mysterious southern continent.

Recent accounts have suggested the city of Moonspire has disappeared altogether. It is not known whether this was the result of destructive forces, or if it was a defensive measure. 

[[Thraine Axegrinder]] recently spoke with [[Remyr Ulth]], a woman purportedly from Moonspire, who suggested Moonspire may not be gone after all. She claims the city narrowly escaped conquest, but does not know its location.
