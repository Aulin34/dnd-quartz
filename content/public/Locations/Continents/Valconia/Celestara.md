The [[Queen of Moonspire]] noted the importance of this city in the independence and survival of the Valconia altogether.

Nesting Celestara away from the rest of the continent is a cursed valley within a treacherous mountain range, home to mysterious dwarves.

Celestara has long been at war with the neighboring region of [[The Burning Expanse]], home to hellspawn. The primary defense from these creatures is the arcane wall, which was imbued with Fey magic from [[The World Tree]].

Since the destruction of The World Tree, the fate of Celestara has become dire. It's unknown as to the current status of their battle against The Burning Expanse, but by all accounts they've remained strong in the face of annihilation.
