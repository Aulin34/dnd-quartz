The Cinderforge is one of the wealthiest kingdoms in the realm, blessed with mineral-rich mountains that provide an endless supply of steel and iron. This abundance of resources has made Cinderforge a center of industry and wealth. The cities of Druecia have long sought to trade with Cinderforge, but most attempts have been blocked by the Kingdom of Almera.

Cinderforge is ruled by the Scorched King, a powerful dwarf whose authority is absolute. Under his leadership, Cinderforge commands one of the strongest armies and most industrialized economies in the realm. The dwarves of Cinderforge are hardy and resilient; most work in the mines, extracting precious minerals and magical materials for the kingdom.

Despite its strength and wealth, Cinderforge prefers to remain isolated from foreign affairs. The one major exception is its trade relationship with the Kingdom of Almera. While Cinderforge is rich in minerals, its rugged, mountainous terrain makes farming difficult. As a result, the kingdom relies heavily on Almera for food and agricultural supplies.

Laws in Cinderforge are strict and unforgiving. Crimes are met with harsh punishment, and those who break the law are sent to Blackreach, a notorious island prison from which few ever return. The people of Cinderforge value order and discipline, believing that strength and self-reliance are the keys to their kingdom's survival.
