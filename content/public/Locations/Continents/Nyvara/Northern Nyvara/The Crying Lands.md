> # The Crying Lands
> ![[cryinglands_icon.jpg]]

When the explosion destroyed the northern half of Lunaris Point three years ago, nearly a million lives were lost. The devastation left behind more than just ruins—it left an echo of suffering. Survivors claim that even now, they can still hear the faint cries and screams of the dead carried on the ocean breeze. This haunted stretch of land has since become known as The Crying Lands.

The Crying Lands are completely uninhabitable. A mysterious green gas blankets the area, burning to the touch and killing all life that once thrived there. Nothing grows in the Crying Lands, and no creature dares to linger. Despite the city’s efforts to contain the toxic gas, it continues to seep from the massive crater left behind by the explosion.

In desperation, the city offered a massive reward to anyone brave enough to venture into the crater and uncover the source of the gas. A handful of adventurers have tried—but none returned. Their bodies were never recovered, presumed consumed by the poison that now stains the land.

A brave group of adventurers, including members of Druecia's Finest, ventured into The Crying Lands to unveil the secrecy behind the explosion. They discovered a harrowing labyrinth underneath its surface, which contained elaborate traps monitored by robots.

It seems the explosion, the labyrinth, and the robots within were all the result of a mad scientist - [[The Creator]].

While The Creator attempted to save the life of his father, The Ice King, he obliterated half of [[Lunaris Point]]. The Empire had sent him there to live the rest of his days underground after he discovered the truth behind [[Celine Emberfall]].
