> # Windmere Emerald Mines
> ![[emeraldmines_icon.jpg]]

Just beyond the city walls, the Windmere family discovered a massive ravine cutting through the earth, its jagged walls glittering with rich, bright green emeralds emerging from the crust. The discovery sparked a massive mining operation, with the Windmeres eager to exploit the valuable resource.

The Windmeres employed dwarves to work the mines, but strange reports have emerged from deep within the ravine. Some of the dwarves have gone missing without a trace, and those who managed to return speak of unnatural sounds and eerie sensations in the depths. Whispers of something ancient and restless lurking beneath the surface have begun to spread among the miners.

Despite the growing fear, the Windmeres remain determined to uncover the ravine’s secrets—and its riches. They've begun recruiting adventurers, offering a substantial reward to anyone brave enough to venture into the depths and uncover the truth about what lies hidden beneath the emerald-lined walls.
