> # Fort Brinsmead
> ![[fortbrinsmead_icon.jpg]]

Between the human colonies and Gull's Landing lies a massive military base, serving as the central checkpoint for nearly all trade between the colonies and the port city. The base is heavily fortified, with well-supplied and well-fed troops stationed there. For years, the forces here have been carrying out calculated attacks on Vesh-Tarra villages — careful not to escalate too quickly but steadily securing key strategic positions to fuel further colonial expansion.

The base is commanded by powerful orc clans, hired by the human kingdoms to aid in the pillaging of the surrounding villages. These orc warlords have earned a fearsome reputation for their ruthless efficiency and brutal tactics. Under their leadership, the base has become a stronghold of both military strength and colonial ambition, slowly tightening its grip on Vesh-Tarra lands.
