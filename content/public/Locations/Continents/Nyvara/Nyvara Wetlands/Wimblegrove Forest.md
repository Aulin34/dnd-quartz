Wimblegrove Forest is renowned for its unique and diverse species that inhabit its dense, winding paths. The forest is home to numerous clans and tribes of various creatures, including bugbears, centaurs, and other mysterious beings. While there are no established cities or villages within the forest, these tribes have made their home among the towering trees and tangled underbrush. The forest is an untamed, wild place, and most travelers avoid venturing into its depths, opting instead to navigate the river that runs to the north, bypassing the forest entirely.

However, in recent years, the cloisters to the east have set their sights on settling new towns and cities within Wimblegrove. These settlers, driven by ambition and the desire to expand their territory, threaten to encroach on the land that has long been home to the indigenous tribes. The tribes of the forest, aware of the looming threat, are increasingly fearful for their future. They see the expanding settlements as a danger to their way of life and the sacred balance they share with the forest spirits.

Centuries ago, Wimblegrove was a very different place—a desolate wasteland, ravaged and scarred by the actions of an evil god. This deity, in its malevolent power, sought to punish the creatures of the land, deeming them too brutish, too different, and unworthy of life. The once-vibrant forest was reduced to a barren, lifeless landscape, and the tribes that had once thrived there were scattered or driven into hiding. The god's wrath seemed unstoppable, and the forest's future appeared bleak.
