> # Mysterious Stone Tower
> ![[stonetower_icon.jpg]]

Deep within the dense, shadowy forests beyond [[Grimrest]] stands a mysterious stone tower, shrouded in rumor and superstition. Though [[Grimrest]] officials adamantly deny its existence, whispers and unsettling tales circulate persistently among the local population and neighboring towns.

According to local legend, life around the tower withers and dies, leaving the surrounding forest lifeless and haunted. It is whispered that [[Grimrest]]'s rulers struck a dark bargain long ago, agreeing to imprison criminals within the tower as offerings for ancient spirits to feed upon. In return, these malevolent entities spare [[Grimrest]] from their wrath, ensuring the kingdom's safety from supernatural forces.

Only one man, Jericho Freeman, claims to have ventured inside the tower and returned. He recounts chilling tales of horrors beyond comprehension—corpses reanimated by demonic possession, moving through dim halls and worshiping indescribable, otherworldly beings. Jericho insists he himself fell victim to possession, plagued nightly by terrifying visions compelling him to return.

Though most locals dismiss Jericho as eccentric or mad, his tales continue to spread, cementing the tower as a pervasive local nightmare. Whether fact or fiction, the stone tower remains a chilling mystery—its shadow looming ominously over the forests of [[Grimrest]], feeding fears and fascination alike.
