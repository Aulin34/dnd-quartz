**REDACTED** - Day-to-day life continues much as it always has: quiet, simple, and predictable. While some outsiders may call this way of life boring, the people of Springrest have always found comfort in their peaceful routines and modest joys.

But that peace has recently been shattered.

Without warning, mysterious attacks have begun to strike the city from the sea. No one knows the cause. The people are terrified—confused as to why their peaceful town, far from war and politics, would become a target. There are far more hostile and threatening places in the realm, and yet Springrest is the one under siege.

Panic has spread like wildfire. In desperation, the citizens have called upon [[Faywharf]] for aid, pleading for protection or even an explanation. But so far, [[Faywharf]] has remained silent—a silence that grows louder and more troubling with each passing day.

What once was a haven of stability now teeters on the edge of fear, as the people of Springrest await answers... or another attack.
