Feylin City was established by [[Faywharf]] as a sanctuary for Fey creatures and their kin—a safe haven from the persecution they face under the harsh rule of the Druecian Empire. While the city does offer protection, this sanctuary comes at a cost. [[Faywharf]] demands significant resources and tribute from Feylin City, leading some to believe that the Fey are simply being exploited in a different way.

Despite this uneasy arrangement, many of the city's residents are grateful for the protection, knowing that life outside the city walls could mean death or enslavement. But beneath the surface, tensions are rising.

The people of Feylin City—primarily Fey, elves, and other magical beings—are increasingly outraged by the Druecian Empire’s brutal actions. Many families have been torn apart, with loved ones imprisoned, killed, or scattered across the realm. Fear for the future grows heavier with each passing day.

In these dark times, hope is found in the worship of Suave, a mystical figure revered as the Great Savior. The faithful believe that Suave will one day rise to liberate their people, restore the power of the Fey, and bring balance and justice back to the world—especially to the elves, who many see as rightful stewards of magic and nature.

Feylin City remains a place of beauty and sorrow, of hope and quiet rage—a flickering ember of resistance in a world growing ever colder.
