Bleakshire lives up to its name—a town abandoned, broken, and forgotten. Once a small human settlement, it was slowly deserted as its prospects dwindled. The final blow came when humans gave up on the town entirely, leaving it to rot.

In their absence, castaways from [[Europa]] and [[Faywharf]]—those deemed "undesirable" or "dangerous"—drifted in. Now, the town is home primarily to goblins, tieflings, and other outcasts who had nowhere else to go.

With no laws, no leadership, and no resources, Bleakshire has become a chaotic sprawl of scavenging and infighting. Every day is a struggle for survival, and the streets are ruled more by desperation than order.

And yet, from the rubble, a sliver of hope has emerged.

A brave goblin named Jeremy has begun to speak of rebuilding—of uniting the people, restoring pride, and turning Bleakshire into something more than just a shadow of failure. Against all odds, he has gained a small following.

That said, Jeremy has been stabbed twelve times since making his first speech.

But his spirit is unshakable.

Bandaged and battered, Jeremy continues to preach his vision of a brighter Bleakshire, refusing to give up until the town lives up to something more than just its name.
