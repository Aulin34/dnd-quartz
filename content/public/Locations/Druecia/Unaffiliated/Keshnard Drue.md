Keshnard Drue has grown into a magnificent city, thriving against all odds in the heart of an arid desert. Its prosperity is made possible by a life-giving river that flows down from the north, nourishing the land and making agriculture and fishing not only possible, but bountiful.

Though originally founded by humans, the city has become a vibrant melting pot, attracting desert-dwelling peoples such as kobolds and Githzerai. These groups have integrated into the city's expanding culture and economy, contributing to its unique identity.

Keshnard Drue serves as a crucial trade partner to both [[Honos]] and [[Knightscross]], acting as a vital commercial hub for the Druecian Empire. Despite repeated attempts by the Empire to formally annex the city, the leaders of Keshnard Drue have declined every offer. The city values its independence and remains firmly committed to solving its own internal challenges rather than becoming entangled in global politics.

The people of Keshnard Drue are deeply in tune with nature, believing the river to be a sacred gift. One of their most honored traditions is a rite of passage for young men, who at the age of sixteen embark on a spiritual expedition. They trek the length of the river through the harsh desert, consuming little food and water. This period of fasting and exertion induces powerful hallucinations, which are believed to be visions sent by nature itself.

When the young men return, their visions are accepted as divine guidance. Whatever path nature reveals—be it warrior, farmer, healer, or wanderer—is honored by the community without question.
