Guthram lies on the edge of the desert, where the blistering heat meets the turbulent waves of the open sea. Life in this brutal climate is sustained only by the bounty of the nearby ocean, with fish being the city’s primary food source. Despite the extreme conditions, the city’s inhabitants—mainly kobolds and Githzerai—have adapted and endure with remarkable resilience.
