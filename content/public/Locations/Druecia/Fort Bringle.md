> # Fort Bringle
> ![[fortbringle_icon.jpg]]
> ##### Information
> | |  |
> | ---- | ---- |
> | Kingdom | Honos |

Once known as Bringleberg, this formerly peaceful town was transformed into a strategic military stronghold during the brutal conflict between humans and elves for control over Druecia. Positioned near a formidable mountain range that blocks easy passage to the southern lands, Bringleberg became the key defensive point preventing elven forces from advancing southward.

The town’s defining moment came during a pivotal battle, when a mere fifty human defenders held the fort against an overwhelming force of seven hundred elven warriors. The fight was savage and desperate, but thanks to the valor of three legendary human warriors, the defenders managed to hold the line long enough for reinforcements to arrive and launch a successful counteroffensive.

Today, the fort still stands as a solemn monument to human resilience and defiance. It symbolizes the might of mortals in the face of what many view as the tyranny of magic-wielding foes. The memory of Bringleberg’s last stand continues to inspire pride and patriotism, especially among those who revere humanity’s triumph over the once-dominant magical races.
