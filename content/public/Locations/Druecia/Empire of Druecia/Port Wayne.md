Port Wayne is less of an actual port and more accurately described as a shady marketplace known for illicit trade and rampant scamming. Although technically under the jurisdiction of [[Albia]], Port Wayne consistently disregards the kingdom's official trade regulations. This disregard makes the city an ideal location for trading dangerous and contraband goods without scrutiny. Interestingly, many major kingdoms quietly exploit this lack of oversight, funneling their questionable dealings through Port Wayne to keep these transactions hidden from official Druecian records and the watchful eyes of rival leaders. Even Cylus of [[Midvault]] frequently imports peculiar and exotic plants through Port Wayne, further emphasizing its role in illicit commerce.

The city itself is notoriously perilous, especially after dark. Residents strongly advise visitors to avoid the streets at night, as local law enforcement is minimal and ineffectual, often turning a blind eye to criminal activities. Merchants in Port Wayne have a widespread reputation for deceit, frequently scamming newcomers and inflating prices up to four times their true value. Overall, Port Wayne symbolizes yet another blemish on [[Albia]]'s already waning influence in the southern region of Druecia.
