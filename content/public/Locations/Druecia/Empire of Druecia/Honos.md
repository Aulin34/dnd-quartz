Honos, often referred to as the Shadowkeep, is the second most powerful kingdom in the [[Empire of Druecia]] and serves as its industrial powerhouse. The people of Honos are fiercely proud, believing that their kingdom is the rightful heart of the empire. Many argue that the capital should have been established in the Shadowkeep itself, given Honos’s vital contributions.

Honos supplies the vast majority of steel and manufactured goods used by the other kingdoms to wage war and expand their dominion. During the brutal war against [[Neathshara]] and the elven forces, it was Honos that bore the heaviest burden—shedding the most blood, leading the most battles, and ultimately conquering the crystalline forest. It was also Honos who delivered the final blow that ended elven resistance in [[Neathshara]].

However, when the time came to decide the future of the empire, the other kingdoms voted to place [[Autumngrove]] in the seat of leadership. Furthermore, the territories Honos had claimed in the crystalline forest were handed over to the newly independent [[Novastra]], stripping Honos of what it believed was its rightful reward. Seeing diminishing benefit in holding the remote lands of [[Neathshara]], the leaders of Honos ultimately relinquished control, but resentment remains strong.
