Cornhall was founded with a singular purpose: farming. Every resident of Cornhall is, in some way, involved in agriculture. It is a land of endless fields, fertile soil, and tireless workers whose lives revolve entirely around growing food for the [[Empire of Druecia]].

As the population of the empire has rapidly expanded, Cornhall’s importance has grown exponentially. The region now supplies a massive portion of the empire’s produce, feeding cities near and far. Without Cornhall’s continual agricultural output, widespread food shortages would occur, sparking what many fear could be an empire-wide crisis.

Though its citizens live humble lives and rarely engage in politics or warfare, the farmers of Cornhall are seen as silent pillars of the empire’s survival. The land is so vital that it is under constant protection by imperial forces to prevent sabotage or invasion. Any threat to Cornhall is treated as a threat to the entire realm.

Despite its simplicity, Cornhall has a quiet pride. The people may not wield swords or magic, but their contribution to the empire is unmatched. They are the lifeblood of Druecia—nurturing the soil that sustains the kingdoms.
