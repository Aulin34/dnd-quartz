Belmouth is less a traditional city and more a militarized outpost and mining settlement, strategically established by the Kingdom of [[Grimrest]]. Its primary purposes are the rigorous training of [[Grimrest]]'s elite soldiers and the extraction of valuable mineral resources. [[Grimrest]], having grown substantially in strength due to the support of [[Autumngrove]], dedicates considerable resources from Belmouth toward trade and funding the colonies in distant Nyvara.

Belmouth serves as a crucial training ground not only for [[Grimrest]]’s own troops but also for soldiers from [[Autumngrove]], preparing them for the defense and expansion of Druecia’s colonial holdings. The training regimen at Belmouth is notoriously severe—soldiers endure intense physical trials and are regularly tasked with grueling labor within the mines, ensuring the city remains economically self-sufficient.

Much of Belmouth’s military practices remain concealed from other kingdoms within the empire, as its extreme methods might provoke alarm or controversy. Consequently, the city exists as a shadowy yet essential cornerstone of Druecia’s colonial ambitions, quietly shaping warriors capable of safeguarding the empire’s expanding borders at any cost.
