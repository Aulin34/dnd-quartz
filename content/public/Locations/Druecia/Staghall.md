Staghall is a charming, modest city under the direct governance of [[Autumngrove]], serving as the agricultural heartland that supplies most of the imperial capital’s crops. Strategically nestled at the crossroads between [[Autumngrove]], [[Novastra]], and [[Honos]] (Shadowkeep), Staghall thrives as a crucial trade hub, where goods regularly flow between these prominent cities.

Life in Staghall is tranquil and peaceful, shaped by the rhythm of hardworking farmers who form the backbone of its economy. Due to its invaluable contributions in agriculture and trade, Staghall enjoys favorable treatment and goodwill from neighboring kingdoms and travelers alike.

Though often overlooked by tourists drawn to more grandiose locations, those familiar with the region recognize Staghall as a hidden gem. The city offers outstanding local cuisine celebrated by food enthusiasts across Druecia. Its welcoming taverns are particularly beloved, featuring talented local bards, friendly faces, and a cozy, authentic atmosphere that provides visitors with a refreshingly intimate experience.
