
### Overview
Artificers who follow the path of the Botanist blend herbalism, arcane chemistry, and mechanical ingenuity. They channel the latent power of nature through cultivated creations—walking terrariums, vine-bound reagents, and syringes full of floral magic. Botanists are masters of magical botany, seeing the natural world not as a thing to protect, but as a resource to understand, adapt, and unleash.

## 3rd Level: Botanical Savant
You gain proficiency in Nature and with the Herbalism Kit. You learn the *Druidcraft* cantrip. You may use an Herbalism Kit as a spellcasting focus for your Artificer spells.

You also gain access to the following always-prepared spells:

| Artificer Level | Spells |
|:---|:---|
| 3rd | *Entangle, Goodberry* |
| 5th | *Barkskin, Spike Growth* |
| 9th | *Plant Growth, Revivify* |
| 13th | *Grasping Vine, Freedom of Movement* |
| 17th | *Tree Stride, Wall of Thorns* |

## 3rd Level: Verdant Companion
You cultivate a botanical construct, known as a Verdant Companion, to assist you.

- **Type:** Plant
- **Size:** Small
- **Armor Class:** 13 + your proficiency bonus
- **Hit Points:** 5 x your Artificer level
- **Speed:** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.
- **Ability Scores:** STR 10, DEX 14, CON 14, INT 4, WIS 12, CHA 6
- **Saving Throws:** Proficient in Dexterity and Constitution saves.
- **Damage Resistances:** Poison, Psychic
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned

### Actions
- **Vine Slash:** Melee Weapon Attack, your spell attack modifier to hit. Hit: 1d6 + your proficiency bonus slashing damage.
- **Spore Infusion:** As part of its attack, your Verdant Companion deals an additional 1d8 + your proficiency bonus poison or necrotic damage (your choice).
- **Special Ability:** Thorn Whip (1/day) as a bonus action.

### Command
On your turn, you can command the Verdant Companion to take the Attack action as a bonus action. If you are incapacitated, it acts on its own, defending itself.

If reduced to 0 hit points, the Companion crumbles into mulch. You can revive it during a long rest or by expending a 1st-level spell slot over 10 minutes.

It regenerates hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier at the start of your turn if it ends its turn within 10 feet of natural terrain (plants, soil, or similar environments).

## 5th Level: Bloomcraft Formulas
Through careful botanical experimentation, you can craft magical herbal concoctions during a long rest. You can create a number of Bloomcraft Formulas equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). Formulas last until your next long rest or until consumed.

**Formulas:**
- **Sunburst Salve:** As an action, a creature gains 2d6 + your Artificer level temporary hit points.
- **Brambleroot Bomb:** As an action, you can throw a bomb (range 30 ft.). Creatures within a 10-foot radius must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your spell DC or become restrained until the end of their next turn. Starting at 11th level, restrained creatures also take 1d6 piercing damage.
- **Sporetrail Capsule:** As a bonus action, throw the capsule on the ground. You leave behind a **10-foot-radius cloud** of thick, shimmering spores that lasts 1 round:
	  - Any creature inside gains half-cover
	  - Any creature who enters or starts there must succeed a Dex save or have **disadvantage** on their next attack.
- **Ironstem Infusion:** As a bonus action, a creature gains **resistance to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage** until the start of their next turn.

At 20th level, you can store up to 5 formulas at once and craft 2 per long rest for free.

---

## 9th Level: Reactive Growth
When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to cause grasping vines to erupt around them. The attacker must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or be restrained until the start of your next turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1), and regain all uses after a long rest.

You and your Verdant Companion ignore non-magical difficult terrain caused by plant growth.

---

## 15th Level: Master Herbalist
Your expertise in botanical magic reaches its peak.

- **Living Laboratory:** You can create a mobile magical greenhouse during a long rest. Within it, downtime crafting of potions is halved in time and gold cost.
- **Regenerative Flora:** When a creature you can see within 30 feet drops to 0 hit points, you can cause rapid plant growth to stabilize them and restore 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier hit points. You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
- **Vinewalk:** You can cast *Tree Stride* once without expending a spell slot. You regain this ability after a short or long rest.

---

## 20th Level: Apotheosis of Bloom
You become a true master of botanical magic.

- **Photosynthetic Surge:** Whenever you cast a spell that restores hit points or grants temporary hit points, you also regain hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier.
- **Bloomcraft Mastery:** You can store up to 5 Bloomcraft Formulas at once and craft 2 for free daily.
- **One With the Garden:** Upon death, a magical seed appears where you fell. After 1 minute, if the seed is not destroyed, you resurrect at that location with half your maximum hit points. This ability can be used once, and resets after 7 days.

# 🌿 Botanist-Themed Magic Items

## Uncommon
- **Vial of Living Spores:** Release a cloud of spores that grants temporary hit points.
- **Photosynthetic Cloak:** Regain hit points when exposed to direct sunlight.
- **Mossweave Gloves:** Boost healing spells by +2 healing per die.

## Rare
- **Rootstep Boots:** Gain burrow speed through soil and soft earth.
- **Botanist's Belt of Blooming:** Automatically cultivate minor herbs and reagents daily.
- **Myconid Syringe:** Inject spores into allies or enemies for temporary effects.

## Very Rare
- **Seed of Resurrection:** Store a creature’s soul in a magical seed for revival.
- **Evergrowth Staff:** Cast *Plant Growth* and *Wall of Thorns* without using spell slots.
